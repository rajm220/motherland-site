THE ACHE

Everyone abroad is missing something small

Dear home — it's not the big things I miss. It's the exact brand of pickle amma buys, the murukku from the shop near the bus stand, the soap that smells like your bathroom. Nobody abroad sells these. Nobody abroad even knows to look.

— every second-year abroad, probably

Right now, getting it there means:

  • ✕Begging a relative to carry 4 kg of snacks in their check-in bag
  • ✕Hoping a stranger on Instagram ships what they promised
  • ✕Guessing whether it'll even clear customs
  • ✕Paying whatever price is quoted that day, in cash, no receipt

Motherland is the fix: a standing, accountable channel between India and everywhere its people have scattered to — verified sourcing, transparent pricing, tracked delivery.

TRACKING

From your message to their doorstep

Every order moves through the same four stops — batched with others heading the same way, to keep shipping honest and affordable.

04

Tracked courier, straight to the door — with a story worth telling.
